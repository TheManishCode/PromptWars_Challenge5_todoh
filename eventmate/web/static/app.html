                        Sending

                        Pausing holds every message that would leave, in every plan of this workspace: ones you approved, ones sent under a standing permission, and the emails to you. Nothing is lost: approved messages wait. An approval lasts 48 hours; one older than that is not sent and needs a fresh draft. Page watches keep reading public pages while paused; they send nothing, and the emails about what they find are held too. Resuming sends what is waiting, so it shows you exactly that list first.

                          Releasing lifts the pause for this exact list, across every plan in this workspace, and then each message goes through the same checks as any other: the daily and per-vendor limits, the plan still matching the text, the do-not-contact list and the 48-hour approval. Some may wait or be stopped, so the number is how many are queued, not how many will be sent. Anything approved after this goes out as a normal approval.

                          Automatic messages only

                          Stops follow-up questions sent under a standing permission, the emails to you and page watches. Messages you approve yourself still go out. This is separate from pausing: pausing never changes it, and resuming never lifts it.

                          Standing permission

                          Do not contact

                          An address on this list is never contacted, on any plan or job, and anything already queued to it is stopped. Only you can take one off.
