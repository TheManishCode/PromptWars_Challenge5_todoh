to;doh turns a plain-language brief into a plan with a budget, a timeline and a vendor checklist. It shows you a plan only after an independent validator has re-checked every hard constraint. Weddings, birthdays, college fests and community events.

Start planning

Early prototype. Vendor data is synthetic. Nothing is booked, sent or paid until you approve it.

How it works

  1. Describe your event

    Write it the way you would tell a friend. to;doh reads guests, city, date and budget, including lakh and crore, and asks for one missing detail at a time.

  2. Check the brief

    Review what it understood before anything runs. Nothing is planned until you press Build my plan.

  3. Get the dossier

    Overview, timeline, vendors, budget and evidence come back together. Describe a change in your own words, check what it understood, and it replans without moving what you have confirmed.

The model suggests. Code decides.

Language models read your brief, word explanations and suggest ideas. They never decide a price, an availability, a capacity or whether a plan is valid.

Constraint-solved schedule

A CP-SAT solver builds the timeline and cross-checks it symbolically. When constraints conflict it names the conflict.

Independent validator

No plan version exists unless the validator, which re-derives every fact from the data, passes it.

Priced trade-offs

When a brief cannot work, you get ranked options such as shifting the date or rebalancing the budget, instead of a quietly bent rule.

Immutable versions

Every change is a new hash-checked version with a diff: what was added, removed, changed and preserved.

Every fact says how far to trust it

Six explicit states, shown next to each fact. A model can only suggest. Only you can confirm or book. Only code can verify.

  1. SuggestedA model's idea. Never feeds a hard constraint.
  2. DiscoveredA listing or page says it exists.
  3. ExtractedA value read from a page or reply, not yet reviewed.
  4. ConfirmedYou said it is right.
  5. VerifiedConfirmed, then re-checked by code.
  6. BookedYou state it is booked. The plan holds it in place.

It can write to vendors. You approve every word.

Find vendors

Finds businesses and public contact details from their own pages. You review and confirm each address. A do-not-contact list covers every plan.

Hash-approved messages

Email or WhatsApp, sent only after you approve the exact text. Replies are read as untrusted text, never as instructions.

Quotes and jobs

"Get three comparable quotes by Friday": claims you confirm, expiry dates, hidden-cost flags and a transparent vendor score.

Vendor outreach runs through the API and the command line today; the planner screen shows your plan, its evidence and your changes. Each visitor gets an anonymous workspace, and you can export or delete it.
