## Live program (branch live/integration, not merged to main)

### LM1: a quote prices only the event it fits (audit A1)
- `VendorRepository.price(provider, guests, event_date)` and `quote_expired(...)` use the newest quote that fits the event (`agents/vendor.py: quote_fits`): same `quoted_for_date`, and for a per-plate price the guest count quoted or inside the vendor's own min/max. Anything else is history and the seed or catalogue price applies. All callers pass the event date.
- `promote_quote` takes the reply's own quote (`ObservationStore.quote_for_reply`), not the vendor's latest, and refuses a quote given for another date.
- A quote confirmed through a follow-up job also records the plan's event date. A quote typed in through the API (`POST /api/observations/<vendor>/quotes`) prices only if it names `quoted_for_date`.
- A quote confirmed from a document line is recorded for a plan's event date when the plan is named (`confirm_quote_line(..., plan_id)`, the page already sends it); without one the date is unknown and it never prices (D-013).
- Tests: `tests/test_quote_event_fit.py` (reviewer probes `cc49526` plus fit and expiry cases), `tests/test_quote_promote.py`. Controls: date check removed (2 fail), guest check removed (1 fails), promote on latest (1 fails).

### LM2: the wrong event is never guessed; a brief survives into its plan (audit A2, A4)
- A reply with none of our thread headers is matched by the sender's address only when one event was ever written to at that address (`match_method` = `sender`, shown as "Matched by the sender's address, not by the thread"). When two or more events were, it is held in a review queue (`messaging/reply_review.py`, `GET /api/reply-reviews`, `POST .../assign` and `.../dismiss`, a "Which event?" note on the Replies step) and filed nowhere until the user picks. WhatsApp has no thread headers, so it takes this road. Every stored reply carries its method (`header`, `sender`, `assigned`, `pasted`); `PollResult.held` counts the held ones.
- `planning/brief_links.py`: when a brief is built into a plan the link is recorded and the outreach, inbound, shortlist and quote-confirmation stores resolve either id to the same files, so a reply after the build is the plan's reply and can be read and confirmed from the plan. `_scope(brief)` reads as the plan once there is one. A pending brief with a request still unanswered is neither expired nor swept, and a reply keeps the brief (`quotes-carry` is done).
- Tests: `tests/test_reply_matching.py` (8; the reviewer's A2 probe is the first). Controls: guess the newest (3 fail), no store alias (1), no sweep protection (1), method not recorded (4).

### LM3: replies are not missed silently; status words say what is known (audit A3, A5)
- `EmailChannel.poll` asks the IMAP server for the threads we know (`FROM` each address written to, `HEADER In-Reply-To` / `References` each of our message ids, delivery failure notices), up to 200 messages a poll newest first, plus the newest 20 other messages of the window for stop requests and notices. `last_poll` reports examined, fetched, overflow and mode; the poll result carries `overflow`, `spam` and `warnings`, and the Replies step shows them ("N more replies are in the mailbox and were not read this time"). A server that cannot search by thread falls back to the window and says so.
- Optional spam folder (`EVENTMATE_IMAP_SPAM_FOLDER`, empty by default): read-only, only addresses we wrote to, the results go to the review list as `spam` messages (a note on the Replies step) and are never filed, read as quotes or obeyed.
- A5: each request carries `delivery` (`draft`, `queued`, `accepted`, `bounced`, `replied`, `self`, ...) with a label that does not claim delivery; the Message step, the overview and the dashboard use it.
- `tests/mailfake.py` FakeIMAP now answers SEARCH (SINCE, FROM, HEADER) and keeps folders, and fails if a mailbox is opened for writing. `tests/test_mail_poll.py` (12 tests). Controls: newest-fifty again (4 fail), overflow not counted (1), spam filed like inbox (1), spam read without being named (1).
- New variable, placeholder only: `EVENTMATE_IMAP_SPAM_FOLDER` in `.env.example`, `deploy/env.example` (empty) and appended, empty, to the local `.env`.
- R-019: filing a held reply is compare-and-set under the review store's cross-process lock (`ReplyReviewStore.claim`): open, then assigning (a lease of 5 minutes, naming the event), then assigned. Two callers at once: one wins. A claim that never finishes lapses and can only be finished for the same event. Tests: a two-process race, a second tab, a lapsed claim; controls: no claim in assign (4 fail), claim not exclusive (2 fail).

### LM4: OAuth sign-in for the mailbox (audit A6, WP2 auth)

- `python -m eventmate.messaging.smoke_email` follows the chosen route (for OAuth it asks Google for one access token and sends no mail).
- `docs/INTEGRATIONS.md` is new: every Google fact used, its URL and the date it was read (2026-11-09), including the 500-a-day sending limit, the app-password caveats and the refresh-token expiry rules; and what is NOT verified. `docs/EMAIL_SETUP.md` has exact steps for a dedicated free mailbox, revoke and isolation.

### WP4: deposits and balances (ledger first, providers second)
- `eventmate/payments/`: `terms` (total, deposit, balance, due dates, payee name and method, the vendor's own sentence about the deposit, status proposed / confirmed / deposit_paid / paid / refunded / cancelled; deposit plus balance must equal the total; UPI and PayPal handles validated; eight or more digits in a row refused, so no account or card number is kept), `approvals` (a payment proposal with a hash of plan, terms and version, kind, amount, currency, payee, method, handle and quote; 24 hour expiry; an append-only event log of every step and every attempt), `desk` (the rules), `providers` (`PaymentProvider`: create_instruction, status, reconcile).
- Rules, each shown to fail when removed: terms only from a quote confirmed in full; one live payment per (terms, kind); the deposit before the balance; approval refused on a wrong hash, after expiry, or when the terms changed; an approved payment is carried out once (a cross-process claim); a provider failure is final for that proposal; paid is recorded only when a named person says so and only then reaches the budget ledger; refunds cannot exceed what was paid; a hosted visitor's workspace never uses a provider.
- Providers: `ManualProvider` (default: says exactly what to pay, to whom, how; moves nothing) and `PayPalSandboxProvider` (flag `EVENTMATE_PAYPAL_MODE=sandbox` plus sandbox credentials; fixed sandbox host; no live mode; the proposal id is its idempotency key). UPI links and gateways are **not built**: the specification could not be read.
- Compare shows each quote's deposit beside the total (never added to it). A built plan's page has "Deposits and balances": set up terms from a chosen quote, confirm who is paid, propose, approve exactly, "Show me how to pay", "I paid", refunds and the log, with the statement that the app moves no money and guarantees no delivery.
- Routes under `/api/plans/<id>/payments` (terms, propose, approve, instruct, status, paid, failed, cancel, refund, note). `docs/INTEGRATIONS.md` has every PayPal, UPI and Razorpay fact read, with URLs, dates and what is unverified. `docs/BLOCKED.md` has the owner's questions.
- Tests: `tests/test_payments.py` (26) and the hosted journey (set up, confirm, propose, approve, how to pay, axe clean, I paid). Controls: hash not checked (2 fail), no expiry (1), duplicate live payments (1), paid not in the ledger (3), provider in a visitor workspace (1), stale terms accepted (1), hash without the amount (2).
- New variables, placeholders only: `EVENTMATE_PAYPAL_MODE`, `EVENTMATE_PAYPAL_CLIENT_ID`, `EVENTMATE_PAYPAL_CLIENT_SECRET` (`.env.example`, `deploy/env.example`, appended empty to the local `.env`). Real payment, sandbox round trip: NOT RUN.
- R-020 (found by the reviewer: a double payment): a lost answer is no longer a failure. `ProviderOutcomeUnknown` (a connection that failed or timed out after the payout was posted, a 5xx, 429 or 408, or an accepted answer with no batch id) makes the proposal `outcome_unknown`, which stays in the live set: no second payment for the same terms can be proposed, approved or carried out, and nothing is posted again. Only 4xx refusals are plain failures, and they free the slot. The user settles an unknown outcome with "It went through" (records paid, with the reference, into the ledger) or "It did not go through" (records failed), each with their name. A payment claimed and then abandoned by a dead process becomes unknown after a 5-minute lease (`settle_stuck`, run on every payment action and view), never failed. The provider lookup by our own sender batch id is NOT claimed (the endpoint was not found in the pages read); `find` says where to look in the sandbox. Tests: the reviewer's scenario (exactly one payout POST, a second propose refused) for a reset, 500, 503 and 429; settle either way; the dead process. Controls: reset mapped to a plain failure (2 fail), unknown outside the live set (5), dead process called failed (1), 5xx and 429 as plain failures (3).
- R-021: the freshness re-check at carry-out is tested: an approval older than 24 hours is refused, becomes expired and the provider is not asked (not even for a token); terms that moved on after the approval cancel it. Control: re-check removed (2 fail).
