## Why not 9 everywhere
Four of the gaps are not code: a real screen reader, a real phone, a real model key and an independent tester. They are listed above with the cost to close each; claiming a 9 without them would not survive a strict judge.

## Stage
Release candidate. Built, tested, containerised, monitored, with CI that has been linted but not yet run on GitHub. Not deployed. Owner decisions before going public: licences of three models, secrets, TLS proxy, alert routing (`docs/RELEASE.md`).
